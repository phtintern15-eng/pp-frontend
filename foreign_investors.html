Clarity & Compliance

NRI & Foreign Buyer FAQ

Key legal, FEMA repatriation, and Embassy Power of Attorney answers for overseas residents.

Can NRIs and OCIs buy unlimited residential and commercial properties?

Full permission to acquire residential properties.

How can I repatriate rental income and sales proceeds back to my foreign bank account?

Rental yields can be freely repatriated post local tax deduction (TDS) via your NRO account with Form 15CA & 15CB from our Chartered Accountants. For property sales, capital proceeds up to USD $1,000,000 per financial year can be remitted abroad freely under the FEMA Liberalized Remittance Scheme.

Is physical presence required in India for registry and paperwork?

No. You can execute a Special Power of Attorney (SPOA) from your home country, attested at the Indian Embassy or Consulate. Our authorized legal team signs on your behalf at the Sub-Registrar's office, and original verified title deeds are couriered to your foreign residence.

Can I get a home loan or mortgage in India as an NRI?

Up to 80% financing available.

What documents do I need to prepare before starting?

Passport, PAN, address proof, and NRE/NRO.
